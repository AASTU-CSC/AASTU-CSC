 **Student-led Cybersecurity Club**

---

## About Us

The **AASTU Cyber Security Club (AASTU CSC)** is a student-led community based at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University. We focus on:

- Ethical hacking & cybersecurity training  
- Hosting and solving Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges  
- Promoting cyber awareness and team projects  

Whether you're a beginner or advanced, you're welcome to learn, share, and grow with us!
